## The problem

Job seekers send the same CV to every job, and generic advice doesn't tell them what to change for a specific role.

## What was built

- **Upload a CV and a job description**, and the AI returns specific improvements for that role.
- A **FastAPI** backend calling the **OpenAI API**, with a **React** front end.
