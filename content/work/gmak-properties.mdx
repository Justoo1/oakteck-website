## The problem

A real-estate business needs buyers and tenants to find properties online, and its team needs every enquiry, contract and property tracked in one place behind the site.

## What was built

- **A marketing site with listings and search**, built with Next.js and Tailwind.
- **An Odoo backend** for leads, contracts and properties, so enquiries from the site land where the team works.
