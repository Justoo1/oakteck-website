## The problem

A business selling on both Shopify and WooCommerce has one stock count in Odoo but two shops taking orders. When orders are copied across by hand, or one at a time, the same item can sell twice and the ERP falls behind.

## What was built

- **Async middleware** that syncs Shopify and WooCommerce into Odoo, running on FastAPI with Celery workers.
- **Redis locks** around stock updates, so two channels can't sell the same last item.
- **A six-view operations dashboard** in Next.js, so the team can see what is syncing and what needs attention.
